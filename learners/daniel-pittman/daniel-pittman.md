# Daniel Pittman

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Sciences at Metropolitan State University of Denver and Co-Director of the Community-Centered Computing (C3) Lab. I lead research initiatives that leverage Data Science, Machine Learning, and Large Language Models to address pressing societal challenges. As Principal Investigator of the NSF-funded Sustainability Hub (a five-institution Colorado partnership), my work focuses on democratizing sustainability data to empower communities and support evidence-based decision-making. My research spans sustainability, cybersecurity, Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), with applications in environmental stewardship, mental health, and community well-being. Prior to academia, I developed extensive industry expertise as a software engineer and architect, and I am a Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP).
