# Shannon Boldt


I am a member of the Lummi Nation and a Native Environmental undergrad student attending the Northwest Indian College in Bellingham Washington. Employed for the last 30 years as a Data Mangager for the PNPTC, consortium and work for the Jamestown and Port Gamble S'Klallam tribes in the Seattle area. I belong to the Observing Unci Maka workgroup that is funded by ESIIL. We are working on our second year of our grant studying the effects of legacy mining on the Pine Ridge and Hopi reservations.
