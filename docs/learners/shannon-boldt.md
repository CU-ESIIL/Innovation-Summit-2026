# Shannon Boldt


I am a member of the Lummi Nation and a Native Environmental undergrad student attending the Northwest Indian College in Bellingham Washington. Employed for the last 30 years as a Data Mangager for the PNPTC, that is a natureal resource consortium for the Jamestown and Port Gamble S'Klallam tribes in the Seattle area. I belong to the Observing Unci Maka working group that is funded by ESIIL. We are working on our second year of our grant studying the effects of legacy mining on the Pine Ridge and Hopi reservations. I am focusing on Environmental Justice on tribal lands.
